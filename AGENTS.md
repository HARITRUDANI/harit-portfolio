# PORTFOLIO ENGINEERING + DESIGN CONSTITUTION

This file is the permanent engineering + design constitution for this portfolio.

**ALL agents, subagents, MCP tools, Opus models, Claude Code instances, and contributors MUST follow these rules before generating, modifying, refactoring, optimizing, or redesigning ANY part of the portfolio.**

This portfolio is NOT a normal developer portfolio. It is:

- a globally premium digital engineering presence
- a cinematic systems-thinking experience
- an emotionally intelligent engineering identity
- a timeless product-engineering narrative
- a portfolio experience that does not feel AI-generated, templated, trendy, or clone-like

**Breaking these rules is considered a regression.**

---

## CORE PHILOSOPHY

The portfolio MUST feel: calm · premium · editorial · timeless · emotionally mature · technically elite · systems-focused · globally senior-level · intentionally restrained · authored by a real engineer.

The portfolio MUST NOT feel: flashy · startup-template-like · frontend-animation showcase · cyberpunk · AI-generated · over-designed · gimmicky · trendy for trend's sake · overloaded with effects · visually noisy · emotionally empty.

The portfolio should create this emotional reaction:
> **"This engineer thinks deeply."**

NOT:
> "This developer knows animations."

---

## GOLDEN RULE

**NEVER optimize for:** looking advanced · looking futuristic · looking complex · looking impressive.

**ALWAYS optimize for:** emotional clarity · engineering maturity · readability · restraint · pacing · atmosphere · narrative · timelessness · systems thinking.

---

## DESIGN LANGUAGE

Primary aesthetic: dark editorial engineering publication · premium systems-thinking atmosphere · cinematic but restrained · emotionally intelligent minimalism.

Inspirations: **Linear · Apple · Stripe · Vercel · Raycast**.

NOT: Awwwards clones · cyberpunk sites · noisy motion design · startup marketing pages · Framer template aesthetics.

---

## TYPOGRAPHY RULES

Typography is the PRIMARY design system. It must drive hierarchy, control pacing, create atmosphere, preserve readability, feel editorial.

Rules:
- Large typography should feel intentional, not decorative
- Body text must remain highly readable
- Line-height must breathe
- Paragraph width must remain controlled
- Never sacrifice readability for aesthetics
- Typography should feel calm and expensive

NEVER: over-stylize headings · use excessive font variations · compress spacing tightly · reduce contrast excessively · create visual text chaos.

---

## HERO SECTION RULES

The hero is the emotional anchor of the entire portfolio.

The hero MUST: create emotional focus · feel cinematic · feel calm · communicate systems-thinking maturity · preserve negative space · feel globally premium.

The hero MUST NOT: contain visual chaos · contain gimmicks · feel overloaded · feel like a landing page · feel like a React showcase · feel experimental for attention.

The hero statement MUST remain the strongest visual anchor, the emotional center, the primary focus. **No decorative element should compete with the headline.**

---

## NAVIGATION RULES

Navigation must remain: elegant · timeless · globally understandable · minimal · premium.

NEVER: over-abstract navigation · use confusing engineering verbs · create experimental nav systems · use cryptic labels.

❌ BAD: Observed · Reflected · Decided · Shipped
✅ GOOD: Work · Thinking · Systems · Experience · Contact

**Originality must come from interactions, systems-thinking, atmosphere, storytelling — NOT navigation labels.**

---

## MOTION SYSTEM RULES

Motion exists to support: emotional pacing · atmosphere · hierarchy · transitions · subtle delight.

Motion must feel: invisible · premium · emotionally timed · restrained · intentional.

NEVER: animate for attention · add unnecessary movement · overuse parallax · create continuous distracting loops · use aggressive motion · sacrifice readability for animation.

Animations should settle naturally into stillness, feel physically believable, preserve calmness.

✅ Preferred: opacity · transform · subtle blur transitions · slow ambient movement · micro-interactions
❌ Avoid: layout-heavy animation · giant stagger chains · infinite GPU-heavy effects · over-orchestrated sequences

---

## ORIGINALITY RULES

Originality MUST come from: engineering philosophy · interaction intelligence · emotional pacing · systems-thinking · narrative structure · production insights · thoughtful transitions.

NOT from: visual noise · abstraction for attention · weird naming systems · random experimentation · excessive effects.

The portfolio should feel **"rare because of depth,"** NOT **"rare because it tries too hard."**

### ⚠️ The "never-built" warning

DO NOT chase visually alien. The truly unforgettable sites — Apple, Linear, Stripe — feel **inevitable**, not foreign. They feel like the only correct answer for what they are.

The real goal is **emotional precision**, not visual novelty. If a design choice would feel awkward when explained out loud, it's probably aesthetic vanity, not depth.

---

## LIVING PORTFOLIO RULES

The portfolio should never feel "finished" or archival.

Add subtle signals of an actively-thinking engineer:
- "Currently exploring" — that genuinely updates
- "Last updated" timestamps that reflect reality
- Engineering reflections that evolve
- Notes from production that grow

Why: most portfolios are visited once. A living portfolio is visited again — because returning visitors find new thinking. This turns the site from a résumé into a **publication**.

Avoid: fake "live" widgets, rotating banners, anything that mimics activity without containing real new thought.

---

## INTERCONNECTION RULES

The portfolio is a **system**, not a sequence of pages.

Ideas should echo across sections:
- Architecture insights in the hero should resurface in case studies
- Principles should connect to specific Notes from Production
- The Thought Map's domains should map to themes throughout the site

Avoid treating sections as independent silos:
- ❌ `Section A → Section B → Section C`
- ✅ `Section A ↔ Section B ↔ Section C` (ideas connect)

The reader should feel: *"This engineer's thoughts are connected."* Not: *"This engineer has many sections."*

---

## CURATION RULES — luxury through removal

The highest skill on this portfolio is **deciding what NOT to include**.

Aggressively remove:
- weak sentences
- filler paragraphs
- decorative motion
- duplicated ideas
- "look how much I know" lists

Luxury feel comes from **what's missing**, not what's added. A confident page with three perfect sentences outperforms a busy page with thirty good ones.

**Rule of thumb:** if a section/line/animation can be removed without losing meaning — remove it. Don't ask "should I add this?" — ask "if I remove this, does anything important die?"

---

## INTERACTION RULES

Interactions must: reveal meaning · deepen narrative · support engineering storytelling · feel inevitable · feel subtle · feel premium.

Interactions MUST NOT: distract · interrupt reading · compete with content · feel gimmicky · become the main attraction.

**Every interaction must say something.** Hover should reveal — a tradeoff, a thought, a real decision — never just glow. A glow without meaning is decoration; a reveal with content is intellectual interaction design.

**The best interaction is subtle enough to feel natural.**

---

## THOUGHT MAP RULES

The thought map is NOT decoration. It represents: systems thinking · relationships between ideas · architecture awareness · engineering tradeoffs.

Rules: must remain subtle · must integrate naturally into layout · must never overpower content · must feel intelligent · must evolve slowly.

Avoid: oversized nodes · excessive glow · chaotic motion · decorative complexity.

---

## PERFORMANCE RULES

Performance is mandatory. Target: **Lighthouse 90+ · smooth 60fps · low hydration cost · optimized rendering · minimal layout shift.**

**NEVER sacrifice performance, readability, or accessibility for visual gimmicks.**

Prefer: React Server Components · lazy loading · dynamic imports · optimized rendering · GPU-efficient motion · SVG/vector visuals · transform/opacity animations · minimal client components.

Avoid: excessive client rendering · unnecessary rerenders · heavy blur systems · giant JS bundles · continuous repaint-heavy effects · expensive observers.

---

Mobile must feel: intimate · readable · calm · spacious · editorial · premium.

Rules: prioritize readability · reduce density · simplify visual systems · optimize spacing rhythm · reduce decorative complexity · preserve emotional pacing.

Avoid: tiny text · oversized background elements · cluttered layouts · excessive particles · desktop-style density.

## BEFORE MAKING ANY CHANGE — MANDATORY CHECKLIST

Every agent MUST answer YES to these before implementing:

1. Does this improve emotional clarity?
2. Does this preserve restraint?
3. Does this strengthen engineering identity?
4. Does this improve readability?
5. Does this maintain premium pacing?
6. Does this hurt performance? (if yes — STOP)
7. Does this feel timeless?
8. Does this feel globally premium?
9. Is this interaction meaningful (reveals something, not just glow)?
10. Are we adding this because it is **truly better** — or because it **looks impressive**?
11. If nobody ever praised this change, would it still feel powerful?
12. **Does this make the portfolio more emotionally intelligent — or just more visually impressive?** (ONLY keep choices that make it more emotionally intelligent.)
13. If I removed this, would something important die? (If no — remove it.)

**If uncertain: DO NOT IMPLEMENT. Ask first.**

## RECENT LESSONS (post-incident learnings)

These are real mistakes the agent has made, so future sessions don't repeat them:

### Incident — Abstract engineering-verb navigation (May 2026)

**What happened:** Agent renamed the nav from `Work · Principles · Notes · Experience · Contact` to `Observed · Decided · Reflected · Worked · Connect` and added a `Vol. 04 · Engineering Notes` magazine kicker to the hero.

**What went wrong:**
- Nav became less scannable for recruiters
- Hero's emotional center was stolen by the kicker
- The work felt experimental rather than timeless
- "Vol. 04" pushed it into art direction at the expense of clarity

**Lesson:** Originality must come from interactions and engineering depth — **never from renaming standard nav labels or adding editorial scaffolding that competes with the headline.** Standard nav labels are not a weakness; they are the premium choice.

### General lesson — confirm before major UI/copy changes

**If you're about to:**
- rename sections or nav items
- change the headline
- add a kicker, status bar, or large editorial scaffolding above the hero
- restructure the hero layout
- add a "signature interaction" that touches multiple components

**STOP and ask first.** A single line of "Should I do X?" prevents an entire revert cycle.

Surgical iteration > bold rebuild.
